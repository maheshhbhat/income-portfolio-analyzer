Disclaimer: Yields and growth rates shown are illustrative, static estimates updated manually — not real-time market data and not a forecast of future returns. Growth figures in particular are deliberately conservative, long-run baselines rather than an extrapolation of any security's recent performance — they may understate how well a security has actually been doing lately, by design, so the tool doesn't implicitly promise that a hot streak will continue. The year-by-year projection applies these static rates deterministically every year (no market volatility, no sequence-of-returns risk, and no Monte Carlo simulation). The withdrawal amount grows every year by the flat, illustrative inflation rate you enter (year 1 = the amount you requested, later years compound from it) — actual inflation varies year to year and is not modeled here. This tool does not model taxes, fees, or rebalancing, and is for educational purposes only; it does not constitute investment advice.
